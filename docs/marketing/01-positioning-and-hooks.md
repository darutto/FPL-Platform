# Positioning statement and hooks

Status: draft for review (cerebro / Leo). Every claim is mapped to a product fact at the bottom.

## Positioning statement (ES primary)

> **FPL Asistente te explica el porqué de cada decisión.**
> Capitán, transferencias, chips y calendario: respuestas con datos reales de FPL y el contexto para entenderlas, en español.

English version (for later):

> **FPL Asistente explains the why behind every decision.**
> Captain, transfers, chips and fixtures: answers built on real FPL data, with the context to understand them.

### Expanded (for website / Patreon page)

For Spanish-speaking FPL managers who want to decide with confidence, FPL Asistente by Bendito Fantasy is an AI assistant that turns FPL data into clear explanations: why this captain, why this transfer, why now for a chip. It's made by the Bendito Fantasy community you already listen to every week, so the reasoning sounds like the podcast, available whenever you need it.

**Core idea: explanation, not prediction.** We don't promise to beat FPL's own numbers. We promise you'll understand them and own your decision.

## Three hooks

Each hook is the first 1–2 seconds of a short video, the opening line of a post, or a podcast mention.

### Hook 1: "Entiende tu capitán en 10 segundos"
- **Angle:** speed plus clarity before the deadline.
- **Shows:** a captain question, then the captain card showing form, xG involvement, fixture and minutes risk, each with a one-line reason.
- **Line:** *"No te digo a quién poner. Te enseño por qué."*

### Hook 2: "¿Dónde dispara y dónde le hacen gol al rival?"
- **Angle:** a visual insight most FPL tools don't show.
- **Shows:** the zones card. The player's shot map overlaid on the next opponent's conceded zones, with the matchup opportunity lighting up.
- **Line:** *"La oportunidad está donde se cruzan los dos mapas."*

### Hook 3: "Tu equipo, tu pregunta"
- **Angle:** personal, about your own team rather than generic tips.
- **Shows:** your FPL squad on the pitch, tap a player, "Ask AI", then a transfer check with a clear reason.
- **Line:** *"Toca a cualquier jugador y pregúntale a la IA."*

### Reserve hook (once probable XI ships)
- *"El once probable antes del deadline, y quién entra si falta un titular."* Only use this after the feature is live (see claims below).

## Tone rules
- Positive and opportunity-framed. Show what you gain, not what others get wrong.
- Chip names stay in English: Wildcard, Bench Boost, Free Hit, Triple Captain.
- Example players: use current PL players and no stat claims we can't show on screen. Don't use Salah.
- Bendito Fantasy voice: warm, community, a bit of humour.
