# Four-week launch plan (gameweek rhythm)

Status: draft for review. The budget assumption is **€0 paid media**. Everything uses owned channels (podcast/YouTube, benditofantasy site, community) plus organic short-form video.

## Weekly rhythm (repeat every gameweek)

| Day | Moment | Action |
|---|---|---|
| GW review day (after last match) | Podcast GW review | 30–60s on-air segment: "we asked FPL Asistente about…". Show the card on screen in the YouTube version. |
| Mid-week | Planning window | Short: hook video (transfers / fixture calendar / zones). |
| Deadline −1 day | Captain decision | Short: "Capitán GW__ explicado" captain card series. **Anchor content.** |
| Deadline day (morning) | Last-minute | Community post: "5 free questions today, use them before the deadline." Link with UTM. |
| After deadline | Engagement | Poll: "¿A quién capitaneaste?" with results next podcast. |

## Week by week

### Week 1: Launch to the existing community
- **Goal:** first 50 logins from podcast listeners.
- Podcast episode opener: Leo demos the app live (screen share on YouTube) and introduces the wide-screen workspace.
- Pinned YouTube comment and description link with UTM `utm_source=podcast&utm_campaign=launch_w1`.
- Website banner on benditofantasy: "Prueba FPL Asistente, 5 preguntas gratis al día".
- Short #1: Hook 3 "Tu equipo, tu pregunta".

### Week 2: Show the depth
- **Goal:** users try more than one card type.
- Shorts: Hook 1 (captain) and Hook 2 (zones).
- Podcast segment: listener question answered live by the app ("send us your doubt, we ask the assistant").
- Collect 2–3 user quotes for social proof (with permission).

### Week 3: Chips and fixtures
- **Goal:** move from one-off questions to a weekly habit.
- Short: fixture calendar with the 5/8/10/12-GW windows and streaks, framed as "plan your Wildcard / Bench Boost".
- Podcast: chip strategy segment built on the calendar view.
- First Patreon conversion push (see doc 03): "the free tier is for trying; the Patreon tier is your weekly co-pilot".

### Week 4: Community and conversion
- **Goal:** first paying subscribers.
- Share community results: "the community's captain picks this GW".
- Short: the best-performing hook from weeks 1–3, re-cut.
- Podcast: thank early users, announce what's coming (probable XI with licensed data) as a **reason to subscribe now**, without claiming it's live.

## Measurement

| Metric | Source | Week-4 target (first pass) |
|---|---|---|
| Logins (new) | App auth logs | 100 |
| Users asking 3+ questions | Backend logs | 30 |
| Patreon paid conversions | Patreon | 5 |
| Short completion rate | TikTok/YouTube analytics | >40% |
| Clicks per channel | UTM | podcast vs. shorts vs. site |

These targets are assumptions to calibrate after week 1.
