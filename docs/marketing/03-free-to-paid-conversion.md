# Free-to-paid conversion idea

Status: draft for review. Tier details need confirming with Leo against the actual Patreon setup.

## Principle

**Free shows the value, and paid makes it a weekly habit.** Free users should get a complete, impressive answer every time. They are limited in how much they can ask, not in how good the answers are.

## Proposed split

| | Free (Patreon login) | Paid (Patreon) |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | ~5/day (current) | More / higher limit (current paid tiers) |
| Cards | All card types, full quality | All card types |
| Squad view + Ask AI | ✅ (it is the hook) | ✅ |
| Fixture calendar | 5-GW window | 5/8/10/12-GW windows + streak highlights |
| Zones card | 1 per day as a "taste" | Unlimited |
| Wide-screen workspace | View | Full |
| Coming: probable XI, replacements, position changes | Preview / teaser | ✅ first access |
| Community | — | Name on the podcast, early-access feedback group |

Calendar windows and zones gating are **proposals** and would need a product change. Leo decides.

## Upgrade moments (where we ask)

1. **When the free limit is hit:** "You've used your 5 questions. The deadline is in X h. Unlock more with Patreon." Show the deadline countdown to make it timely and positive.
2. **On the 8/10/12-GW calendar tab:** a blurred preview plus "plan your Wildcard with the long view".
3. **Pre-deadline captain card:** small footer, "Patreon members get unlimited follow-ups."
4. **Podcast:** "Patreon supports the show *and* gets you the assistant", which combines two reasons in one.

## Offer ideas (no cost)
- **Founders tier label** for the first subscribers (badge, named on the podcast).
- **First month framed around a chip:** "Subscribe for your Wildcard week."
- **Referral:** a listener who brings a friend gets extra free questions (needs a product change; later).

## What to measure
- % of free users who hit the daily limit (the strongest signal of demand).
- Conversions per upgrade moment (tag each CTA).
- Week-2 return rate of free users.
