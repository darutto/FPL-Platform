## Grid semantics (criterion b, question 13)

Format: `formation_field = "row:col"`, row 1 = goalkeeper, rows rise towards the
attack; `formation_position` is a 1–11 running index. Within a row, `col` is
**absolute along the touchline, not relative to the team**: for the home team
col 1 is the team's right and the highest col its left; for the away team the
numbering is mirrored (col 1 = the team's left). Equivalently, col 1 is the same
physical side of the pitch for both teams.

Evidence (GW6 hand-read over 9 fixtures, plus a GW1–5 defender check):

- Home, right side at col 1: Saka (Arsenal RW), White (RB), Cash (Villa RB),
  Neto (Chelsea, right), Hume (Sunderland RB), Khalaili (Palace RWB).
- Home, left side at the highest col: Calafiori, Kerkez, Shaw, Ruggeri (LBs),
  Madueke (Arsenal LW, col 3 of 3).
- Away mirrored: Truffert (Bournemouth LB), Udogie (Spurs LB), Mykolenko
  (Everton LB), Gvardiol (City LB) at col 1; Adam Smith (RB), Nunes (RB), Kayode
  (RB) at the highest col.
- Mitoma: not among the starters of the 50 fixtures read, so no check possible.
- Systematic check over GW1–GW5, 24 left/right fullback appearances of named
  players (Calafiori, Kerkez, Udogie, Shaw, Robertson, Porro, Reece James,
  Hume, Mukiele) in 4-back rows: **23 of 24** sit at the predicted edge for
  home/away. The exception is Mukiele at col 2 of 4 (probably a centre-back
  role that day).

Caveat: the side of each player comes from their usual role (my knowledge), not
from a provider field; the lineup row has no left/right marker. The inference
holds for the sample but should be re-checked on a few clubs by someone who knows
their shape, and any consumer must encode the home/away flip explicitly
(home/away comes from `formations[].location`).
