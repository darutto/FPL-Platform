**What "compound_or_partial_name" is, concretely** (diagnostic, read off the
pairs by hand): the Sportmonks `name` is the legal name (several given names and two family
names) while its `display_name` is exactly what FPL calls the player. The registry's
`surname_birth_date` tier compares the *last* word of each name, so it
resolves a legal name only when the last word coincides; otherwise the pair has
the same birth date, the same first and family name, and no tier. The other
cases are the FPL element carrying an extra family name the provider omits, word order
reversed between the sources, and short forms.

## Manual confirmations (mechanism, 2026-10-11)

Ten players the matcher left unresolved are put to a human: nine have a clear counterpart in Sportmonks, the
tenth (the loanee below) has none. His confirmations are not applied by hand-editing results: they go in `--out/mapping/manual_pairs.json` (local,
not versioned), one entry per pair with the Sportmonks id, the FPL id, who confirmed, the date and the
evidence. A run validates the file (all five fields, a real date, no id repeated) and applies each pair as the
last step, only over what both automatic passes left unresolved, and refuses it, with a recorded reason, when
the Sportmonks id or the FPL element is unknown, the player is already paired, the element is already resolved
or ambiguous, or the birth dates are absent or differ. An accepted pair has its own method,
`manual_confirmation`, so the report shows how many resolutions are human. An append-only log
(`manual_pairs.log.ndjson`) records what each pair did on each run. The report states the official rate
**before** confirmations (the 95 % gate, automatic) and **after** them (the 99 % post-queue target), side by
side. Three stay unresolved whatever is confirmed (the loanee and the two players absent from every squad), so
the target needs 8 of the 9 with a counterpart confirmed: then four are left and 421 of 425 resolve (99.06 %).

**A case the rule does not cover (R10).** One of the twelve is not a mapping problem: FPL says he "has
joined Como on loan for the rest of the season" and lists him as unavailable. The departure rule needs him
in Sportmonks with a team history and does not find him, so it does not exclude him. The rule is left as it
is during the trial; whether FPL's news line is enough evidence of a departure is to be decided with Leo.
